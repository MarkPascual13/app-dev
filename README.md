# 🦇 My Favorite Movie: Batman
 
## About the Movie
 
**Batman** is one of my favorite superhero movies. 
It tells the story of Bruce Wayne, a billionaire who 
fights crime and protects Gotham City.
 
I like Batman because he is brave, intelligent, 
strong, and determined to protect innocent people.
 
## Main Character
 
- **Bruce Wayne** – The man behind the Batman identity.
- **Batman** – The Dark Knight who protects Gotham City.
- **Alfred Pennyworth** – Bruce Wayne's loyal butler.
- **Commissioner Gordon** – A police officer who helps Batman.
- **The Joker** – One of Batman's most famous enemies.
 
## Why I Like Batman
 
1. Batman has amazing action scenes.
2. He uses intelligence and technology to fight crime.
3. He protects people without having superpowers.
4. The story teaches courage and justice.
 
## My Favorite Quote
 
> It's not who I am underneath, but what I do that defines me.
